# 🧠 Human Curiosity Engine — Deep Architecture Specification

## 1. Philosophical Foundations
The Human Curiosity Engine rejects the premise that AI's primary utility is providing answers. In complex adaptive systems, premature optimization on known answers obscures the catastrophic failure modes of unasked questions.

## 3. Epistemic Separation of Knowledge
Every assertion inside the engine is categorized into:
1. **Known Facts:** Empirically verified historical consensus.
2. **Inferences:** Probabilistic extrapolations from current trends.
3. **Speculations:** High-novelty thought experiments grounded in non-linear dynamics.
4. **Unknown Territory:** Areas where no empirical telemetry exists.
