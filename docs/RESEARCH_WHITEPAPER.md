# 📄 Research Whitepaper: Beyond the Answer Engine

**Title:** Beyond the Answer Engine: Towards Multi-Agent Question Discovery Intelligence  
**Author:** Vijay Mahes (Lead Architect, Human Curiosity Engine)  
**Date:** September 2026

## Abstract
Large language models (LLMs) have achieved unprecedented velocity in answering questions. However, the highest-order bottlenecks in human civilization—existential risk, catastrophic environmental tipping points, and cognitive atrophy—arise not from uncomputed answers, but from **questions that humanity failed to formulate**. This paper presents the architecture of the **Human Curiosity Engine**, a multi-agent cognitive system specifically engineered to interrogate assumptions, identify systemic blindspots, and discover unasked questions across multi-decade horizons.

## 1. The Optimization Trap
When artificial intelligence is trained purely as a conditional answer generator $P(A \mid Q)$, it inherits the implicit biases, horizon myopias, and linguistic blindspots embedded within the prompter's question $Q$.

## 2. Inversion of Inquiry
By shifting the epistemic workflow from $Q \to A$ to $\text{World} \to \text{Blindspots} \to Q^*$, the system acts as an adversarial dialectic mirror for humanity's unexamined assumptions.
